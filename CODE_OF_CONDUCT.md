# Code of Conduct

Ghost Hands is a Ghost Developer Studio project. Keep it simple:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with code and claims, never with people.
  No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs,
  discussions, or anywhere the project shows up.
- **Bring receipts.** Critique with evidence; accept correction with
  grace. "It works on my machine" is a starting point, not an argument.
- **Stay on the work.** Keep discussion about the project, its design,
  and its honest limits.
- **Assume good faith first** — then verify, like everything else here.

Maintainers may moderate, edit, or remove contributions that break
these rules, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations. Report conduct problems privately through the contact route
in `SECURITY.md`.
